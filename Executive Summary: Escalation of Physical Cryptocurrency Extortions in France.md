**Executive Summary: Escalation of Physical Cryptocurrency Extortions in France**

**What happened?**

* Physical extortions, kidnappings, and home invasions targeting cryptocurrency holders in France escalated significantly between January 2025 and August 2026.
* In 2025, the attacks primarily targeted high-profile industry figures. Documented cases include the January 2025 kidnapping and mutilation of Ledger's co-founder, and the May 2025 kidnapping attempts targeting family members of the Paymium CEO and another crypto entrepreneur.
* In 2026, the victim profile expanded to include retail investors, standard executives, and their families. In February 2026, a magistrate and her mother were kidnapped in Isère and sequestered in Drôme due to the magistrate's partner's professional involvement in digital assets.
* On January 1, 2026, the European DAC8 directive mandated the automatic exchange of tax data regarding cryptocurrency holdings.
* In late June 2026, a massive cyberattack breached the French tax authority (DGFiP) database. The attackers extracted data matching taxpayers' identities, physical addresses, and declared cryptocurrency holdings. The French Ministry of Economy and Finance officially confirmed this breach on August 13, 2026.
* Organized crime networks are utilizing this leaked tax and KYC data to conduct targeted home invasions.
* The operational execution of these physical assaults is systematically outsourced by criminal syndicates to young recruits via encrypted messaging platforms.

**What is the impact?**

* France became the global epicenter for physical cryptocurrency extortions in 2026.
* According to the cybersecurity firm CertiK, France accounted for 33 of the 52 globally confirmed cases in the first half of 2026. French legal and security sources recorded up to 77 kidnapping and extortion cases nationwide during the same six-month period.
* The financial exposure resulting from physical cryptocurrency extortions globally reached $124 million in the first half of 2026.
* The physical security of compliant cryptocurrency investors has been severely compromised. The DGFiP data breach exposed the personal and financial data of up to 678,000 taxpayers, removing the anonymity previously associated with digital asset ownership.
* Criminals are successfully bypassing robust digital security measures (such as hardware wallets and multi-factor authentication) by applying extreme physical violence and torture to force victims to authorize transactions manually.

**What is being done & next steps**

* Law enforcement agencies have executed multiple arrests. On March 24, 2026, six suspects were arrested in connection with violent home invasions in Verneuil-sur-Seine and Sallanches. In early August 2026, a police intervention thwarted an active home-jacking attempt in the Yvelines department.
* The French judicial system has shifted its strategy to centralize these investigations. The National Jurisdiction Against Organized Crime (JUNALCO) and Interregional Specialized Jurisdictions (JIRS) are linking isolated attacks to prosecute broader criminal enterprises.
* On March 27, 2026, an investigating judge formally linked the various May 2025 cases, resulting in 45 indictments.
* Cybercrime units are deploying blockchain intelligence tools to trace forced on-chain transfers.
* Authorities are actively coordinating with centralized stablecoin issuers to freeze extorted funds on public ledgers where possible, and monitoring fiat off-ramps to identify the final beneficiaries.
